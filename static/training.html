Train an aiming model

Generate examples, fit a small model and compare its predictions on positions it has not seen.

Lightweight simulation demo. This uses generated two-dimensional target positions and a simple geometric teacher. It does not use incident footage, the Unitree G1, MuJoCo, or real robot controls. Training runs in your browser and is not saved.
